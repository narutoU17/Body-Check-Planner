Tape measurements for a more accurate body fat % (recommended)

With waist and neck (plus hips for women) the tool uses the US Navy tape method, usually within 3 to 4%. Without them it estimates from BMI and age, which can be off by 5% or more for muscular or very lean people.

Track your progress

Weigh yourself in the morning, 3 times a week, and log it here (saved in this browser). After 2 weeks the plan checks your real progress and adjusts your calories, like a coach would.
